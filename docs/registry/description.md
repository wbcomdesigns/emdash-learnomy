Learnomy adds free courses to your EmDash site. Write courses and lessons in EmDash's own editor, put each course's lessons in order, and add a short quiz to any lesson. No payments of any kind.

## What you get

- Courses and lessons under their own menus, with a cover, excerpt and category per course.
- A Course outline page: pick a course, add lessons by title, set their order.
- Open courses anyone can read, or Members courses for signed-in people.
- Free enrollment, Mark complete, progress and a finished state.
- One quiz per lesson: multiple choice or true or false, up to 20 questions, scored on your site so the right answers are never sent early.
- Your courses for members, on your site's account page.
- Enrollments and Quiz results lists with names, and erase for one member.
- A dashboard card, and MCP tools so an AI agent can build a whole course.

## What Members means

Members keeps out guests, not other members. A guest sees a Members lesson's title and a sign-in prompt; the text never reaches their browser or search engines. Anyone signed in to your site can read every published lesson.

## How members join

By your invite: Users, Invite User, role Subscriber. Invites and recovery need an email provider. Self-signup will work once EmDash releases its fix for allowed-domain signup.

## How it fits together

The plugin is the engine. The course pages come as a site kit you copy into your site once. See Installation.
