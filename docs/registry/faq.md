**Is it free?**
Yes. No paid version, and no payments of any kind.

**Does Members hide lessons from other members?**
No. Members keeps out guests, not other members. Anyone signed in can read every published lesson.

**How do people join?**
By your invite: Users, Invite User, role Subscriber. Self-signup will work once EmDash releases its fix for allowed-domain signup.

**Why does it ask to read my content and user accounts?**
Content: to list your courses and lessons by title and show visitors only published ones. User accounts: to show member names in Enrollments and Quiz results. It never writes your content.

**Can one lesson be in two courses?**
Yes. Add it to both outlines.

**Can an AI agent build courses?**
Yes. Turn on Agent access on the plugin card and give the agent a token with the Learnomy MCP tools scope. Courses and lessons use EmDash's own content tools; outlines and quizzes use Learnomy's.

**Are there certificates?**
Yes. A member who finishes a course gets a printable certificate with a link they can share or turn off. You see who finished each course under Certificates.

**What are the limits?**
200 lessons per course, 20 questions per quiz, and course pages look through the first 1,500 published lessons of a site.

**Does it work with my theme?**
The pages take your theme's font, colours and light or dark scheme, and you can restyle them with --learnomy-* tokens.
