# What's new in 0.3.0

**Included in Free.** Learnomy has no paid version.

Course certificates with a link to share. No new permissions and no new storage. After updating, run the site kit command again with `v0.3.0` (see [After every update](../getting-started/install.md#after-every-update)): it adds `src/pages/certificates/[token].astro` and changes Your courses, `learnomy.ts`, `learnomy-strings.ts` and `learnomy.css`.

- **A certificate for every finished course.** When a member finishes a course (every lesson done, and every graded quiz with a pass mark passed), they get a certificate: their name, the course, the completion date and your site's name. It prints cleanly, black on white, and the browser can save it as a PDF.
- **A link to share.** Each certificate has its own long random link. Anyone with the link sees the certificate on your site, which is how they can check it is real. It never shows the member's email. Search engines are asked not to list it.
- **The member decides.** In Your courses each finished course has **View**, **Copy link** and **Turn link off**. A link that is off says "Certificate not found" to everyone. **Turn on with a new link** gives a fresh link; the old one never works again.
- **You see who finished.** **Plugins**, **Certificates**: pick a course to see each member who finished it, the date, and whether their link is on. **Turn link off** stops a link at once.
- **Courses finished before 0.3.0** get their certificate the first time the member opens Your courses or you open the course on the Certificates page. Nothing to run.
- **Erase removes certificates.** A member's own erase and Erase their data both delete the certificate, and its link stops working.
- **Agents** get `listCertificates` and `turnOffCertificate`.
