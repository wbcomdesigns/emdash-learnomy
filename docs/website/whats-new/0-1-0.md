# What's new in 0.1.0

**Included in Free.** Learnomy has no paid version.

The first release of Learnomy for EmDash. Needs EmDash 1.5.0 or later.

New permissions: **Read your content**, to list your courses and lessons by title and show only published ones, and **Read user accounts**, to show member names in your lists.

- **Courses and lessons in EmDash's editor,** under their own Courses and Lessons menus, with a category and cover for each course.
- **Course outline:** pick a course, add its lessons by title, set their order.
- **Open or Members courses.** Members keeps lesson text away from guests and search engines.
- **Free enrollment and progress,** with Mark complete, previous and next lesson, and a finished state.
- **One quiz per lesson,** up to 20 questions, scored on your site.
- **Your courses** on the account page, with progress, Continue and an erase button.
- **Enrollments and Quiz results** with names, find by name or email, and erase for one member.
- **A dashboard card** with counts, New course and Course outline.
- **Agents ready:** eight MCP tools for outlines, quizzes and lists, plus EmDash's own tools for courses and lessons. See [Build a course with agents](../developers/agents.md).
- **Course pages that fit your theme,** with tokens, a wording file and `data-learnomy-*` hooks. See [Make it yours](../customize/make-it-yours.md).

Members join by your invite. See [Let members join](../getting-started/let-members-join.md).
