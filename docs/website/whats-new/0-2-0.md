# What's new in 0.2.0

**Included in Free.** Learnomy has no paid version.

Richer quizzes. No new permissions and no new storage. After updating, run the site kit command again with `v0.2.0` (see [After every update](../getting-started/install.md#after-every-update)): the course and lesson pages, Your courses, `learnomy-strings.ts` and `learnomy.css` changed.

- **Pass marks.** Give a graded quiz a pass mark and members must reach it before they can mark its lesson complete. The course page and Your courses show which quizzes are passed. See [Quizzes](../manage/admin-guide.md#quizzes).
- **More question types.** Multiple answers (pick all that apply), short answer and fill in the blank, marked on your site against the answers you accept, ignoring capitals and extra spaces.
- **Retakes and limits.** Per quiz: unlimited, a maximum number of attempts, or a wait between attempts. Members see attempts left and when they can try again. Each member's attempts are kept, and the best score counts.
- **Erase and limits.** A member's own erase keeps only how many attempts they used, and when, on quizzes with a limit or a wait, so erasing never resets the limit. Erase from the dashboard removes everything.
- **Practice mode.** A practice quiz never blocks progress. After submitting, members see the right answers and the explanation you wrote for each question.
- **Quiz results** shows each member's best score and number of attempts.
- **Agents:** `saveQuiz` and `getQuiz` take the new question types, mode, pass mark and retake policy.

Quizzes and results from 0.1.x keep working with nothing to do: an older quiz is graded, with no pass mark and unlimited retakes, and an older result counts as the first attempt.
