# Let members join

**Included in Free.**

To enroll, mark lessons complete or take a quiz, a visitor needs an account on your site. For now, members join by your invite.

## Invite a member

1. In the dashboard, open **Users** and press **Invite User**.
2. Type the person's email address.
3. For the role, pick **Subscriber**. A Subscriber can read your site, enroll and learn, and nothing more. Never give a learner a higher role.
4. Send the invite. The person gets an email with a link, sets up their sign-in, and can then enroll in any course.

Invite as many people as you like. Each one appears under **Enrollments** once they enroll in a course.

## Your site needs an email provider

Invites and account recovery ("I lost my passkey") are sent by email. Until your site has an email provider, EmDash cannot send them.

Check **Settings**, **Email**. If it does not say "Email provider active", install an email provider plugin from the Registry and select it there. Send yourself a test email before you invite anyone.

## Can people sign up by themselves?

Not yet. EmDash has a setting that lets anyone with an email address at your allowed domains sign up by themselves, but it does not work in EmDash 1.5. EmDash is fixing it (emdash-cms/emdash#4863). Once EmDash releases that fix, self-signup will work with Learnomy and this guide will add the steps.

Until then, invite each member as above.

## What a guest sees

A guest who opens an Open course can read every lesson. A guest who opens a Members course sees the lesson titles and a sign-in prompt that says members join by invitation and to ask you for one. See [What Members means](what-members-means.md).
