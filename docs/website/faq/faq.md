# Frequently asked questions

**Included in Free.**

**Is Learnomy free?**
Yes. There is no paid version, and Learnomy takes no payments: no prices, checkout or coupons.

**Can I sell a course?**
No. Learnomy is for free courses.

**Does Members hide a course from other members?**
No. Members keeps out guests, not other members. Anyone signed in to your site can read every published lesson. See [What Members means](../getting-started/what-members-means.md).

**How do people join?**
By your invite: Users, Invite User, role Subscriber. Self-signup will work once EmDash releases its fix for allowed-domain signup. See [Let members join](../getting-started/let-members-join.md).

**Can one lesson be in two courses?**
Yes. Add it to both outlines. Progress is kept per course.

**Do I need to code?**
Only once, to copy the site kit when you install and after each update. Everything else happens in the dashboard.

**Does it work with my theme?**
The kit takes your theme's font, colours, radius and light or dark scheme. It was checked on the EmDash blog, starter, marketing and portfolio templates. See [Make it yours](../customize/make-it-yours.md).

**Can an AI agent build my courses?**
Yes. See [Build a course with agents](../developers/agents.md).

**Are lesson texts in my site search?**
Course titles and excerpts are. Lesson texts are not.

**Can a quiz stop someone finishing a course?**
Yes, if you give it a pass mark. A member must reach the pass mark on a graded quiz before its lesson counts as complete, and a course finishes when all its lessons are complete. Their best score counts. Practice quizzes never block. See [Quizzes](../manage/admin-guide.md#quizzes).

**Can I limit retakes?**
Yes, per quiz: unlimited, a maximum number of attempts, or a wait between attempts. Members see how many attempts are left and when they can try again.

**Can a member reset an attempt limit by erasing their data?**
No. Their own erase removes their progress, answers and scores but keeps how many attempts they used on quizzes with a limit or a wait. You can reset a member completely with Erase their data on Enrollments or Quiz results.

**Do members see the right answers?**
Only on a practice quiz, after they submit, with your explanation for each question if you wrote one. On a graded quiz they see which answers were right or wrong, never the correct answers.

**Can I add essay questions?**
No. Every question is graded on your site; essays would need someone to mark them.

**Are there certificates?**
Not yet.
