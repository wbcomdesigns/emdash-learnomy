# Frequently asked questions

**Included in Free.**

**Is Learnomy free?**
Yes. There is no paid version, and Learnomy takes no payments: no prices, checkout or coupons.

**Can I sell a course?**
No. Learnomy is for free courses.

**Does Members hide a course from other members?**
No. Members keeps out guests, not other members. Anyone signed in to your site can read every published lesson. See [What Members means](../getting-started/what-members-means.md).

**How do people join?**
By your invite: Users, Invite User, role Subscriber. Self-signup will work once EmDash releases its fix for allowed-domain signup. See [Let members join](../getting-started/let-members-join.md).

**Can one lesson be in two courses?**
Yes. Add it to both outlines. Progress is kept per course.

**Do I need to code?**
Only once, to copy the site kit when you install and after each update. Everything else happens in the dashboard.

**Does it work with my theme?**
The kit takes your theme's font, colours, radius and light or dark scheme. It was checked on the EmDash blog, starter, marketing and portfolio templates. See [Make it yours](../customize/make-it-yours.md).

**Can an AI agent build my courses?**
Yes. See [Build a course with agents](../developers/agents.md).

**Are lesson texts in my site search?**
Course titles and excerpts are. Lesson texts are not.

**Are there certificates?**
Not yet.
